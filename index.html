I am a prosthodontist practising in Lahore, where I have spent over a decade restoring function and form for patients with missing, damaged, or failing teeth.

My clinical work centres on fixed and removable prosthodontics and dental implantology. My academic work, as Assistant Professor of Prosthodontics at Azra Naheed Dental College, keeps me honest — I have to practise the way I teach.

In 2021 I co-founded Smile Hub Clinics in Johar Town with colleagues from endodontics and general practice, where I continue to see patients.

I believe complex dentistry is mostly the patient's decision made well. My job is to make the options, trade-offs, and costs legible enough that the decision is genuinely theirs.

I see patients at Smile Hub Clinics, 599 Main Boulevard, Q Block, Phase 2, Johar Town, Lahore.
